      About Window Seat

      Window Seat is a quiet window onto Japan. Public live cams from around the country rotate on their own while long mixes from TOKYO CHILL LAB play underneath. It is made for leaving on the TV.

      Use it on your TV

      • TV browser: open this page in the browser on your Android TV or Google TV and press Start.
      • Cast: open the page in Chrome on a computer, choose Cast, then Cast tab. Go fullscreen for the best view.
      • Remote: arrows to move, OK to choose, Back to close a panel.
      • Controls: tap the picture or the controls button in the corner, or press OK, Enter or M. While a cam plays they fade after a few seconds; they stay up while paused.
      • Keyboard: N next cam, P pin cam, U mute music, Space play or pause (the cam and the music), F fullscreen.
